# HyperLight

> HyperLight Corporation is a Cambridge, Massachusetts photonics company, founded by Mian Zhang
> out of Harvard University, that designs and manufactures thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN)
> photonic integrated circuits on its patented TFLN Chiplet platform for AI and hyperscale data
> center interconnect, DCI/telecom transport, and test and measurement. HyperLight sells
> semiconductor hardware — transmitter PICs, IQ transmitter PICs and packaged electro-optic
> modulators — and publishes no API, SDK, developer portal or machine-readable specification.

Generated by API Evangelist (https://apievangelist.com) on 2026-08-22 from HyperLight's own public
website. This is an independent third-party profile; it is not published by HyperLight.

## API surface

- No public API. Probed 2026-08-22: /openapi.json, /openapi.yaml, /swagger.json, /v1/openapi.json,
  /api-docs, /docs, /redoc, /graphql and /llms.txt all return HTTP 404 on hyperlightcorp.com and
  www.hyperlightcorp.com; every /.well-known/ path (security.txt, openid-configuration,
  oauth-authorization-server, api-catalog, ai-plugin.json, agent-card.json, agent.json) returns 404;
  and api., docs., developer., dev. and portal.hyperlightcorp.com do not resolve in DNS.
- No first-party SDK or client library. The "hyperlight" packages on npm, PyPI and crates.io belong
  to the unrelated hyperlight-dev sandboxing project and to other projects, not to this company.

## Products

- [IMDD Tx PICs](https://hyperlightcorp.com/products/imdd-tx-pics): Intensity-modulation /
  direct-detection transmitter PICs, up to 448 Gbps per lane.
- [DPIQ Tx PICs](https://hyperlightcorp.com/products/dpiq-tx-pics): Dual-polarization IQ transmitter
  PICs for coherent links at 130, 200 and 260 Gbaud.
- [Packaged Modulators](https://hyperlightcorp.com/products/packaged-modulators): Standalone
  electro-optic modulators with up to 145 GHz bandwidth.
